Privacy Policy

Odyssey Clipping · Last updated November 8, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the Odyssey Clipping website and the Odyssey Clipping Uploader. The uploader is used by the Odyssey Clipping team to send videos to its own TikTok account. The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

What we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (display name, avatar and account ID), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the uploader send videos to that account's inbox, plus the status of each upload.

How it is stored and used

Tokens are stored only on the computer running the uploader. They are never sold or shared, and are used only to upload Odyssey Clipping's own edited videos and check their status. We don't collect information about viewers, followers or anyone else.

Deleting data

"Disconnect" in the uploader deletes the stored tokens immediately, and revoking access in TikTok's settings ends all access.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub or message @odysseyclipping on TikTok.
