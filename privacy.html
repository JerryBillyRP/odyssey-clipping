Privacy Policy

Odyssey Clipping · Last updated October 18, 2026

Who uses the app

Odyssey Clipping is used only by its owner to post to the Odyssey Clipping TikTok account. It has no other users and does not collect information from the public.

What we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the authorizing account (display name and account ID), used to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token that lets the app upload videos to that account, plus the posting options TikTok returns (such as allowed privacy levels).

How it is stored and used

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, are never shared or sold, and are used only to upload Odyssey Clipping's own edited videos and check their posting status. We do not collect data about viewers, followers or anyone else.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings ends all access immediately. The owner can also delete the stored token at any time.
