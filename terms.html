About these terms

These terms cover the Odyssey Clipping website and the Odyssey Clipping Uploader, the desktop app the Odyssey Clipping team uses to send its edited clips to its own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs.

The uploader

The uploader connects a TikTok account with Login with TikTok and sends finished videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. It never publishes on its own: each draft is reviewed and posted in the TikTok app. The uploader is operated by the Odyssey Clipping team for its own account.

Content and permission

We only clip streamers who allow it, for example through an official clipping campaign, and we credit the streamer in every post. Paid campaign clips carry TikTok's paid-partnership disclosure. Creators who want a clip removed can contact us and we will take it down.

TikTok

Use of the uploader is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings or with the uploader's Disconnect button.
